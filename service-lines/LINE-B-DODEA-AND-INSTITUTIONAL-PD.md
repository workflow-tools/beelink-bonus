## 1. Verdict

**Do not pursue DoDEA as a customer.** Not as a deferred idea, not as a "someday when the reorganisation settles" idea. Three independent facts each kill it on their own:

1. **The market is roughly $12,000 per year, globally, for a new entrant.** Across FY2023 to FY2026-to-date, DoDEA awarded 21 small professional-learning actions totalling $455,694. Nineteen of those were delivery orders placed against indefinite-delivery vehicles competed years earlier. Only two were standalone purchase orders a newcomer could have bid: NNSTOY at $38,634 and FIRST at $9,999. That is $48,633 across nearly four years, agency-wide, across 161 schools in 11 countries. (Source: USAspending API, awarding subtier "Department of Defense Education Activity", keyword "professional learning", $1K–$75K band, 2022-10-01 to 2026-08-17.)
2. **A German UG is structurally excluded from the price band where every one of those awards sits.** FAR 19.502-2 requires that each acquisition above the micro-purchase threshold and not over the simplified acquisition threshold *shall* be set aside for small business absent a rule-of-two determination. 13 CFR 121.105 defines a small business concern as one "with a place of business located in the United States." ML Upskill Agents UG has none. Nationality is not the barrier — German firms hold large DoDEA primes, including the student-transport contracts for the Vilseck and Hohenfels military communities — but the $15,000–$350,000 set-aside band is closed, and that band is where education services are bought.
3. **The legal exposure runs to your wife's job, not to your company.** See §2. It is manageable but it is not zero, and it is asymmetric: the downside lands on a federal employee's personal criminal-statute compliance, not on a corporate balance sheet.

**Pursue the adjacent buyers instead, and pursue one of them.** The recommendation is the **University of Maryland Global Campus Europe adjunct faculty route** — specifically the currently-posted Artificial Intelligence adjunct position at Grafenwöhr (Job Ref 10024943). It is an institution that already sits on post, already holds the SpouseWorks / Tuition Assistance / GI Bill funding rails, already handles enrolment and billing, and is exempt from the Army-Europe commercial-solicitation regime that would bind you personally. You are paid as faculty. There is no SAM.gov registration, no NCAGE, no FAR 3.601 question, no Privacy Act contractor status, and no conflict arising from your spouse's employment.

The runner-up is a **single Volkshochschule course in the Amberg / Weiden / Amberg-Sulzbach area**, run once, treated as curriculum proving ground and local credibility rather than as revenue (§6).

**Three things must be said plainly, because they contradict the framing of the enquiry:**

- **Nothing in this line is passive.** A taught course section is several hours a week. A workshop day is an immovable eight-hour block plus travel. There is no configuration of institutional PD that fits 15–25 minutes a day.
- **Nothing in this line uses the Beelink.** Section 508 and the Privacy Act eliminate every learner-facing deployment to a federal buyer, and a VHS or UMGC classroom needs no local inference. If the strategic premise is monetising the inference layer, Line B is not where that happens.
- **The runner-up to all of this is shipping ProPrecept Ireland and ProPrecept RSA**, which are code-complete, in markets you already understand, and require no accreditation, no garrison approval, and no new regulatory surface. Every option below should be benchmarked against that.

## 2. Conflict of interest, stated precisely

This needs agency ethics counsel before any approach to any DoD entity. What follows is the framework, not an opinion.

### Case (a) — today: spouse is a DoDEA employee, you are not

**FAR 3.601 does not bar your company.** The text prohibits the *contracting officer*, not the vendor: "a contracting officer shall not knowingly award a contract to a Government employee or to a business concern or other organization owned or substantially owned or controlled by one or more Government employees." Your spouse's employment does not make her an owner or a controller of a UG whose shares you hold. There is no numeric ownership threshold in the regulation — the test is "substantially owned or controlled," which is a facts-and-circumstances judgment, and Germany's default marital property regime (Zugewinngemeinschaft) keeps spousal assets separate during the marriage.

**The restriction that actually operates runs on her, and it is a criminal statute.** 18 U.S.C. 208 imputes to a Federal employee the financial interests of her spouse. She may not participate personally and substantially, as a Government employee, in any particular matter in which her spouse has a financial interest. Separately, 5 CFR 2635.502 gives her a "covered relationship" with your company on two independent grounds — you are a member of her household, and you are a person for whom her spouse serves as an officer — which means she must consider whether a reasonable person would question her impartiality before participating in a matter involving the company, and must seek an agency-designee determination where it would.

**What this restricts and what it does not.** It restricts *her participation as an employee*. It does not restrict the company's existence, its right to sell to non-DoD buyers, its right to sell to DoD buyers she has nothing to do with, or your right to hold and run it. Waivers exist in both directions: 18 U.S.C. 208(b)(1) permits a written waiver by the appointing official; 5 CFR 2635.502(d) permits written authorization by the agency designee.

**Practical rule.** Never sell to her school, her district, or any organisation where she holds a role, sits on a committee, or is an approving official. Given that DoDEA has just eliminated 21 community-superintendent positions and collapsed decision-making upward, the safest formulation is: never sell to DoWEA Europe at all. Put the exclusion in writing and keep it.

### Case (b) — hypothetical: you become a DoDEA employee

**FAR 3.601 then bars award outright.** Your company would be "owned or substantially owned or controlled by one or more Government employees" on the face of it. The only relief is FAR 3.602, which permits an exception authorised at agency-head or Head of Contracting Activity level and only for "a most compelling reason, such as when the Government's needs cannot reasonably be otherwise met." Teacher training that a dozen US vendors already sell will never meet that standard.

Beyond the contracting bar, you would come under 5 CFR 2635 subpart H (outside employment and activities) and DoD 5500.07-R, the Joint Ethics Regulation, which govern outside employment approval, misuse of position, and endorsement. Those would constrain teaching adults in the same community, marketing to colleagues, and any use of DoD facilities or position.

**The two paths are mutually exclusive and should be sequenced deliberately.** If DoDEA employment is a genuine possibility, build only assets that survive it: the UMGC adjunct appointment and any teacher-paid, non-government-funded offering, both subject to routine outside-employment approval. If the UG is ever to sell to DoDEA, that has to happen before any application.

**Flag:** none of the above is a substitute for a written read from the DoDEA agency ethics official and, for anything touching the garrison, the USAG Bavaria Staff Judge Advocate. Both are free.

## 3. The student-records regime — DoDEA is not a FERPA institution

The common error is to reason about DoDEA as if it were a school district. It is not. DoDEA — now the Department of War Education Activity (DoWEA) following the September 2025 renaming under EO 14347 — is a Department of Defense Field Activity. It has no Department of Education nexus and FERPA does not apply to it.

**The actual authority** is the Privacy Act of 1974, 5 U.S.C. 552a, implemented through the system-of-records notice **DoDEA 26 — Department of Defense Education Activity Educational Records**, published at 76 FR 24001 on 29 April 2011. The SORN's stated authorities are 10 U.S.C. 113, 10 U.S.C. 2164 (DoD domestic dependent schools), 20 U.S.C. 921–932 (overseas defense dependents' education), 29 U.S.C. 794, DoD Directive 1342.20 and 1020.1, and E.O. 9397. FERPA appears nowhere.

**Why this is worse for a vendor, not better.** FERPA has a "school official" exception that lets a district designate a contractor as an official with a legitimate educational interest; vendor exposure under FERPA is contractual and administrative. The Privacy Act has no such exception. Instead, FAR Subpart 24.1 applies. FAR 24.102 provides that where a contract provides for the **operation** of a system of records on individuals, the contractor and its employees are considered employees of the agency **for purposes of the criminal penalties of the Act**. FAR 24.104 makes clauses 52.224-1 and 52.224-2 mandatory in such contracts.

Two precisions matter. First, the criminal-penalty deeming attaches specifically to *operation* — design and development trigger the mandatory clauses but not, on the face of 24.102, the criminal deeming. Second, this is personal exposure, not corporate: it reaches individual employees.

**The design rule this produces is absolute.** Adult training only. Zero student personally identifiable information. Never operate, host, or maintain anything on the agency's behalf that retrieves records by a student's name or identifier. Sell instruction and materials; never sell a system. Note separately that DoDEA-level practice and vendor agreements routinely apply FERPA-equivalent protections as policy — so the correction is legally right and operationally neutral. You do not get a lighter compliance load; you get a differently-labelled and criminally-enforced one.

**Scale, for context:** approximately 66,000 military-connected students in 161 schools across 11 foreign countries, seven states and two territories. That is the entire addressable student population, worldwide.

## 4. How DoD actually buys small training engagements

This is the section that separates a plan from a wish. The thresholds moved on 1 October 2025.

| Instrument | Threshold | Authority | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Micro-purchase | **$15,000** (was $10,000) | FAR 2.101 | No competition required, no solicitation, no contract. Purchase card is "the preferred method to purchase and to pay for micro-purchases" (FAR Subpart 13.2). |
| Simplified acquisition threshold | **$350,000** (was $250,000) | FAR 2.101 | Simplified procedures, but competition and — critically — small-business set-aside apply. |
| Construction carve-out | $2,000 | FAR 2.101 (Davis-Bacon) | Not relevant here. |
| Service Contract Labor Standards carve-out | $2,500 | FAR 2.101 | **Does not bite in Germany** — see below. |
| Commercial training, purchase card as payment method | $25,000 | AFARS 5113.270-90 | A *method of payment*, not a purchase authority — see below. |

**The SCLS carve-out does not apply at Grafenwöhr, and this is worth $12,500.** FAR 22.1003-2 states that the Service Contract Labor Standards statute does not apply to contracts performed outside the United States, and FAR 22.1001 defines "United States" to exclude "any U.S. base or possession within a foreign country." Training delivered at Grafenwöhr or Vilseck is therefore outside SCLS, and the full $15,000 micro-purchase threshold is available rather than the $2,500 services carve-out. (The commonly-cited "bona fide professional employees" exemption is *not* in FAR Subpart 22.10 — do not rely on it.)

**The $25,000 AFARS figure is not what it looks like.** AFARS 5113.270-90 directs: "Use the purchase card as a **method of payment** for all commercial training $25,000 and below." AFARS 8-1 confirms SF-182 (Authorization, Agreement and Certification of Training) is a non-FAR-based training action, that the primary purpose must be to train the attendee, that training from a non-government source "must be commercial-off-the shelf (COTS) to purchase with the GPC," and it cross-references FAR Part 13 without adding any dollar threshold of its own. Between $15,000 and $25,000 you still need a purchase order, FAR 13.104 competition, and the FAR 19.502-2 small-business set-aside. **The genuine no-competition doorway is $15,000, not $25,000.**

**Registration mechanics.** SAM.gov entity registration with a Unique Entity ID is mandatory for any federal award and is free. A non-US entity historically requires an NCAGE code from NATO's Support and Procurement Agency before SAM registration — roughly 3–10 business days for NCAGE, two to four weeks end to end. A 2026 change removes the NCAGE prerequisite for some international entities not seeking DoD financial assistance, but DoD still validates CAGE/NCAGE prior to award under FAR 4.1802 and DFARS Subpart 204.18. Registration is a chore, not a blocker — but it should not be done speculatively. Do it only once a named buyer has said in writing that they intend to pay.

**Who holds the card.** DoDEA runs its own Government Purchase Card programme on GSA SmartPay 3, administered from Germany (DoDEAGPCActionRequest@dodea.edu, +49-631-7106-6696). A single email to that office answers two of the most decision-relevant open questions: what a school-level cardholder's actual single-purchase limit is, and whether the SF-182 process can pay a non-US payee invoicing in euros. It is free and it takes ten minutes.

**None of this rescues the DoDEA case.** The micro-purchase doorway is real, and it is genuinely open to a foreign vendor because the set-aside only attaches above $15,000. But a $15,000 ceiling on a market with roughly $12,000 a year of contestable new-entrant volume, sold into an agency mid-reorganisation with no AI budget line, at a relationship-sale cost of months, is not a business.

## 5. What DoDEA would actually buy — and whether anyone is asking

**What they buy today.** Professional learning is designed and delivered internally by the Teaching and Learning Division and district professional-learning specialists, "job-embedded" and aligned to the Blueprint for Continuous Improvement 2025–2030. The public professional-development services page names only internal programmes and lists no external providers, no catalogue, and no vendor channel. GAO's 2022 review found teacher *time*, not money, to be the top reported barrier. Duty-time PD is additionally bargained: the Federal Education Association, an NEA affiliate, represents DoDEA employees, and a court restored collective-bargaining rights in October 2025 after they were stripped by executive order.

**When they do buy outside, they buy attached to products.** Reading the actual award descriptions in the $455,694 sample: HMH and Benchmark Education (reading intervention resources with product training bundled), CodeHS (COTS seat licences with one to two hours of virtual professional learning), SHAPE America (a national-organisation membership), Navigate360, Prophet Corporation. Larger awards follow the same shape: Catapult Learning Patriot LLC at $2.05M for Alternate Content Standards professional learning, AVID Center at roughly $500K/year, AttainX at $486K and $474K for leadership sessions. These are established US education vendors selling curriculum and licences with training attached. They are not contestable standalone PD engagements.

**Is there demand for AI professional development specifically? No published evidence of funded demand.** No DoDEA/DoWEA AI instruction policy or classroom AI guidance could be located as of August 2026. What exists is participation without policy: nine DoWEA schools were named 2026 Presidential AI Challenge state champions — Bob Hope, Brussels, Daegu, Sevilla, Sigonella, Spangdahlem, Stowers, Stuttgart High School and Zukeran — with projects spanning math instruction, writing development, language learning, news broadcasting and school safety. The Department of War's own AI Strategy (Secretary of War memorandum, 9 January 2026) is a warfighting and technical-talent document and says nothing about K-12 schools or teacher training.

That combination — visible teacher enthusiasm, no policy, no mandate, no budget line, no procurement trigger — is the classic profile of a market that looks open and is not. Model DoWEA AI professional development as unfunded enthusiasm.

**The one genuinely interesting fact underneath, and its caveat.** DoDEA educator pay includes an academic salary lane mechanism: an "Application for Academic Salary Lane Change" supported by transcripts, governed by DoWEA Policy Instruction 99-P-003 (21 June 1999), a Memorandum of Understanding with the Overseas Federation of Teachers (2 August 2001), a time-limits MOU (9 January 1998), and a published credit-hour conversion table. If DoDEA educators can advance pay lanes with graduate credit, they have a permanent personal incentive to buy graduate credit with their own money — which would convert this from a procurement problem into a business-to-consumer one, with no SAM, no FAR, no Privacy Act and no Section 508.

**But the specifics are unverified.** The DoDEA salary page does not state the lane designations, does not define what a "+15" lane means, and contains no language about which accreditors' credit qualifies or whether university *extension* and continuing-education credit counts at all. The governing regime is union-bargained and nearly 27 years old, and union-negotiated credit rules are conservative by construction. Model rejection as the base case. Two free emails settle it: one to DoWEA HR asking whether Policy Instruction 99-P-003 admits extension credit, and one to a university extension partner (Adams State Extended Studies, Fresno Pacific Continuing Education, or University of San Diego Professional & Continuing Education — the last already sells "Project READ for DoDEA/Int'l Educators" at $134 per graduate semester unit) asking what their partner-admission process actually is. Neither has been sent. Until both come back yes, this is a hypothesis, not an opportunity.

### The recommended path: UMGC Europe

UMGC Europe operates roughly 50 locations from a Kaiserslautern headquarters, including Grafenwöhr, Vilseck and Hohenfels, and enrols on the order of 55,000 military-affiliated students annually. It is currently recruiting adjunct faculty in **Artificial Intelligence at Grafenwöhr** (Job Ref 10024943), requiring a master's degree in Artificial Intelligence, Data Science and Analytics or a related field, local applicants only, no UMGC-sponsored logistical support, applicant maintains own legal status.

2. **Does taking paid local employment affect your SOFA status?** AER 210-70 §19.c(2) warns that in most host nations, running a private business or seeking employment on the economy may require a residence permit despite SOFA exemptions, and that loss of or impact to SOFA status may occur. Your status derives from your spouse's DoDEA employment. The posting's own language — "applicant maintains own legal status," "not eligible for UMGC sponsored logistical support" — is the institution disclaiming exactly this risk. Get a written read from the garrison legal assistance office before applying.
3. **What does UMGC Europe actually pay per section?** The $3,000–$4,500 figure is Glassdoor-reported US data and unverified for the Europe Division. Overseas sections are also enrolment-contingent and can be cancelled. One email to UMGC Europe HR in Kaiserslautern settles this and gate 1 simultaneously.

**Also note a conflict you would inherit:** teaching AI for UMGC while separately selling a competing private AI course to the same garrison population is an outside-activity conflict UMGC would likely have views on. Pick one.

### The Volkshochschule route, taken seriously

**How you become a Dozent.** You do not wait for a vacancy. You send an *Initiativbewerbung* to the relevant Fachbereichsleitung with a short Kurskonzept — content, target audience, scope, session structure — have a conversation, and sign a Honorarvertrag for the semester. No teaching qualification is required; "fachliche Kompetenz" is the stated standard. Bavaria's Bayerischer Volkshochschulverband runs a Servicestelle Digitalisierung, and the BayEbFöG funding priority "Digitalisierung und Medienkompetenz" continues in 2026, so digital-skills proposals are actively wanted rather than merely tolerated.

**What it pays.** Rates are per 45-minute Unterrichtseinheit: typically €20–40 in the open programme, above €40 for BAMF-funded courses (the BAMF integration and occupational-language rate was raised to €42.23 per UE in 2022). A Bavarian municipal example (Stadt Schongau) pays EDV instructors €45.00–€60.00 per 90-minute Doppelstunde. A 10-session, 2-UE course is 20 UE — roughly €400–800 for a term's evening commitment.

**Why it is worth doing anyway.** It is not revenue and should not be modelled as revenue. It is the cheapest available way to (a) test the curriculum in front of real adults before proposing it to UMGC or IHK, (b) acquire a named local institution as a teaching reference, and (c) sit in a room of exactly the Mittelstand professionals the VilseckKI positioning targets, without any solicitation-rule exposure because the VHS is the contracting party and the marketer.

**Two German legal facts that constrain it.** First, the BSG *Herrenberg* ruling (B 12 R 3/20 R, 28 June 2022) held that a music-school teacher on a standard Honorarvertrag was in dependent employment regardless of the contract's label. The § 127 SGB IV transitional shield for Honorarlehrkräfte **expires 31 December 2026**; insurance and contribution liability begins 1 January 2027 for any Honorartätigkeit classified as dependent employment. That is roughly four and a half months of runway, not sixteen. Second, self-employed teachers without employees are compulsorily insured in the statutory pension scheme under § 2 SGB VI. Structure teaching as UG-invoiced B2B delivery wherever the institution permits it, and get a Steuerberater opinion on § 2 SGB VI before teaching volume grows.

**A third German constraint that applies to anything you sell yourself rather than through an institution.** § 1 FernUSG requires two elements *cumulatively*: predominant spatial separation between teacher and learner, **and** monitoring of learning success. BGH III ZR 109/24 (12 June 2025) set the second bar very low — the ability to ask questions by chat or email can suffice — and extended the statute's protection to entrepreneurs, not just consumers. BGH III ZR 137/25 (5 February 2026) held that predominantly live-online formats are not distance learning; the analysis cited for it states explicitly that **the decision sets no percentage threshold** and that the test turns on whether real-time interaction characterises the offering as a matter of contractual terms. Without ZFU approval a captured contract is void under § 7 FernUSG and every buyer can reclaim payment. In-person teaching, and teaching delivered through an institution that contracts with the learner, are both outside this problem. A self-paced recorded course sold from Germany is squarely inside it.

### The buyers to skip

- **International-school PD days.** No verified European day rate, a 300–500 km travel radius to the Munich/Frankfurt/Düsseldorf cluster, and outbound consultative selling. It is the least passive option available and the money is unquantified.
- **Army Credentialing Assistance vendor status.** Requires a CAGE code via SAM.gov, "in business at least for 2 years," a business licence, an AIPortal account and a signed Department of the Army MOA — and the AIPortal is hosted on a DoD domain that in practice requires CAC-credentialed access a German UG may be unable to obtain. Funding is restricted to credentials listed in Army COOL, so the product would be reselling CompTIA and AWS exam prep against national incumbents with zero differentiation. (The commonly-quoted $4,000-per-soldier-per-fiscal-year cap does not appear in the Army's own vendor process guide — treat it as [unverified], and check whether it is shared with Tuition Assistance.)
- **Direct SpouseWorks provider status.** School eligibility requires two years of operating history as a school, a location that is not a personal residence, and either accreditation by an agency recognised by the US Department of Education, accreditation by an industry-recognised certification organisation, or licensure by a state board recognised by the Department of Defense. The two-year and non-residence rules are curable in principle; the practical answer is that the money reaches you through an already-approved school (UMGC), not through your own approval. Note also that the benefit funds licences, certifications, associate degrees, and continuing education to *maintain* a licence or certification — a bespoke AI curriculum is not an eligible expense at any accreditation level.

## 7. Section 508, VPAT, and the line between teaching and deploying

Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act requires that information and communication technology (ICT) that federal agencies develop, procure, maintain or use be accessible. The pre-2003 micro-purchase exception no longer exists: federal guidance is explicit that ICT bought through a micro-purchase must still meet Section 508 unless a documented exception applies (FAR Subpart 39.2 governs the acquisition side). DoDEA enforces this at the cardholder stage — a Section 508 micro-purchase training certificate is a prerequisite for DoDEA Government Purchase Card cardholder appointment.

**The distinction that matters commercially:**

| What you sell | Is it ICT? | What conformance costs |
|---|---|---|
| A person delivering a workshop in a room | No — it is a service | Nothing |
| Slides, handouts, PDFs, recordings you hand over | Yes — electronic content | Real headings, alt text, tagged PDFs, captioned video. Hours per deliverable, done once per asset |
| A hosted portal, a student-facing tool, a Beelink-backed model interface | Yes — a full ICT deployment | An Accessibility Conformance Report (the VPAT template, published by ITI, is the standard vehicle), remediation, and ongoing conformance as the product changes |

**Teaching a workshop is not deploying an information system.** That is the single most useful sentence in this section. It means the workshop-plus-accessible-materials shape clears Section 508 at trivial cost, while the teacher-built portal from the classroom-build-lab design study is ICT and would need conformance evidence — and the 508 wall does not disappear by keeping the purchase under $15,000.

If a tool is ever required by a federal buyer, price the accessibility audit and remediation into the quote before quoting, and expect it to exceed the revenue on any engagement this size. In practice this is a further reason to sell instruction, not software, into federal buyers — and it stacks with the Privacy Act analysis in §3, which independently rules out operating any system on the agency's behalf.

DoDEA is worse than baseline right now: a rename, a new director appointed March 2026, elimination of the community-superintendent layer announced April 2026, and live union litigation. **German day-rate benchmarks, for calibration and with the caveat attached.** One Düsseldorf agency lists inhouse AI workshops from €1,900 for a full day on-site (€2,480 netto in its detailed table) and from €1,900 for a remote half-day; the same page places freelance trainers at €800–2,000 per day and large consultancies at €5,000–15,000. Those are one agency's list prices for 15–20 participants under a known brand. An unbranded solo operator should model the freelance band, not the agency band. For contrast, IHK Akademie Ostbayern sells "KI-Manager (IHK)" at €2,590 per participant over roughly four months of live online sessions coordinated through Regensburg and Weiden — proof that local willingness to pay exists, and an argument for delivering *as their Dozent* rather than building a competing enrolment funnel.

**The commoditisation floor.** Anthropic Academy currently lists more than twenty free self-paced courses with completion certificates, including beginner tracks requiring no coding. Hugging Face, Microsoft's Generative AI for Beginners and IBM SkillsBuild are free with credentials. Anything sold as information transfer competes with a better-branded €0 product. What remains sellable is what free content structurally cannot deliver: a scheduled time, a human looking at a specific project, a room of peers, and an invoice or certificate an institution accepts. Sell the container, not the information.

Frame the question precisely: does taking paid adjunct employment on the German economy, as a SOFA family member whose status derives from a spouse's DoDEA employment, affect status or require a residence permit? Cite AER 210-70 §19.c(2) so they know what you have already read.
4. **Email DoDEAGPCActionRequest@dodea.edu** (or call +49-631-7106-6696). Ask two things: what is a school-level cardholder's single-purchase limit, and can the SF-182 process pay a German entity invoicing in euros. This closes the DoDEA question definitively rather than leaving it as a maybe.
5. **Send the two graduate-credit emails.** One to DoWEA HR asking whether Policy Instruction 99-P-003 admits university extension / continuing-education credit for academic salary lane advancement, and whether pre-approval is required. One to a US university extension arm (Adams State Extended Studies, Fresno Pacific CE, USD PCE) asking for the partner-admission process, eligibility requirements, timeline and partner-side fee — none of which is published anywhere. If either comes back no, the teacher-paid credit hypothesis is dead and you have saved months.
6. **Get a written ethics determination for your spouse** from the DoDEA agency ethics official covering 18 U.S.C. 208 and 5 CFR 2635.502, before any approach to any DoD entity. Keep it.
7. **Send one Kurskonzept to a local VHS Fachbereichsleitung** for the autumn or spring programme. Low cost, and it forces the curriculum to exist.
8. **Get a Steuerberater opinion on § 2 SGB VI and § 127 SGB IV** before the 31 December 2026 expiry, specifically on whether institutional teaching should be invoiced by the UG rather than personally.
9. **Do not register in SAM.gov or apply for an NCAGE code** until a named buyer has said in writing that they intend to pay. It is two to four weeks of free paperwork with no option value until then.
